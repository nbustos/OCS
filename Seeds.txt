OCS — CANDIDATE SEEDS FOR SEED-BASED CONNECTIVITY (XCP-D fsLR 91k dtseries)
================================================================================
Labels below are exactly as they appear in the XCP-D atlas label tables (and as
column headers of *_atlas-<ATLAS>_timeseries.tsv). MNI coordinates are parcel
centroids in MNI152NLin6Asym (from atlas-4S356Parcels_space-MNI152NLin6Asym_nodes.tsv).

Atlases available in our XCP-D 0.6.0 outputs:
  4S356  = Schaefer-300 (17-net names) + CIT168 subcortical (28) + HCP thalamus (14)
           + HCP hippocampus/amygdala (4) + cerebellum (10)
  Tian   = Tian subcortex Scale III (50 parcels; striatum/thalamus/amygdala/hipp. subdivisions)
  HCP    = 19 whole CIFTI subcortical structures (coarse)
  Gordon = 333 cortical parcels (not used below)

VERIFIED 2026-11-18 against the atlas tables in our XCP-D 0.6.0 derivatives on cortex:
  - atlas-4S356Parcels_dseg.tsv index+label columns identical
    (md5 of `cut -f1,2` = 69d62e871fa2730bb4730f7da083245b)
  - atlas-Tian_dseg.tsv identical (50 nodes, Scale III; note original typos
    HIP-head-l-l, HIP-body-l, PUT-DA-l)
Seed extraction code selects parcels by LABEL, not index.

Format:  SEED_ID | ATLAS | LABEL(S) [index] | MNI centroid (x,y,z) | notes
Priority: [P] = primary/a priori,  [S] = secondary/exploratory

Alternative VTA definitions (if the CIT168 composite is too SN-contaminated):
  - CIT168 VTA-only probabilistic map (Pauli et al., 2018) — not split out in
    XCP-D's 4S; would need the original atlas resampled to 91k subcortical voxels.
  - Murty et al. (2014, NeuroImage) probabilistic VTA/SN atlas — designed for
    VTA-vs-SN resting-state separation.
  - Either can be applied directly to the dtseries subcortical voxels (no rerun of XCP-D).

VTA caveats:
  - ~10–20 voxels at 2 mm; low tSNR; strong cardiac/respiratory pulsatility near
    the interpeduncular cistern. Use the UNSMOOTHED dtseries (desc-denoised) to
    extract the seed, the smoothed one for the target map.
  - XCP-D drops parcels with poor coverage (NaN column). Check the VTA/HN/MN
    columns in atlas-4S356Parcels_timeseries.tsv for NaNs per run.
  - Expected VTA profile (sanity check): NAc/ventral striatum, vmPFC/mOFC,
    hippocampus, ventral pallidum, hypothalamus.
